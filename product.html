The Sensorial Science & Ritual

Envelop your lips in the unyielding weightlessness of haute French couture. Lumière Velvet Matte delivers cloud-soft saturation that dries to an ethereal velvet veil, blurring fine lines while locking in deep petal hydration.

The Parisian Application Ritual:

1. Define the Cupid's bow using the tapered architectural bullet tip.
2. Smooth the pigment outwards across lower and upper lip cushions.
3. Press gently with a tissue for a soft, blurred Parisian ombre finish or layer for opulent, saturated glamour.

Formulated without parabens, phthalates, synthetic talc, or mineral oil. 100% dermatologically tested for delicate and sensitive lip barriers.

French Red Camellia Seed Oil: Deep lipid replenishment maintaining all-day suppleness without sticky residue.
Hyaluronic Micro-Spheres: Dehydrated spheres that swell upon skin contact, visibly plumping lip cushions.

Full INCI: Dimethicone, Polyglyceryl-2 Triisostearate, Camellia Japonica Seed Oil, Synthetic Wax, Silica, Microcrystalline Wax, Tocopheryl Acetate (Vitamin E), CI 77491, CI 15850, Parfum (Delicate French Vanilla).
