## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges modern Parisian high-fashion editorial rigor with sensorial, warm-skin intimacy. It speaks to a discerning global audience seeking effortless, elevated luxury, curated formulations, and artistic self-expression. 

The visual execution relies on an **editorial minimalist aesthetic with warm sensorial depth**. Rather than cold clinical minimalism, the interface embodies the tactile quality of velvet compacts, polished metallic hardware, and luminous dewy textures. Generous whitespace evokes the restraint of high-fashion print publications, while ultra-fine micro-borders, quiet surface tiers, and deliberate proportions convey bespoke craft. High-contrast typography pairs expressive display serifs with neutral, architectural grotesques to maintain absolute legibility across digital commerce touchpoints.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by cosmetic skin undertones, precious metals, and deep espresso velvet:

- **Primary (`#1A1412` - Velvet Espresso):** A concentrated, near-black umber that replaces stark digital black. Used for high-contrast headlines, primary button fills, and structural outlines to establish an authoritative luxury foundation.
- **Secondary (`#B77A65` - Burnished Rose Gold):** A warm metallic terra-cotta used for secondary accents, active indicators, interactive hover states, key micro-actions, and editorial highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#F7D6CD` - Soft Petal Blush):** A soft cosmetic flush used for seasonal tag backdrops, active swatch outlines, subtle surface washes, and promo callouts.
- **Neutral (`#FDFAF7` - Pristine Alabaster Ivory):** The primary canvas background. Warm, reflective, and softly luminous, avoiding sterile blue-white hues.
- **Supporting Foundation Tones:**
  - `Warm Nude Canvas`: `#F9F5F0` and `#EFE8DF` for alternating editorial modules and card surfaces.
  - `Micro-Border Tone`: `#EADBDB` for gossamer 1px borders that provide structure without visual clutter.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial atmosphere akin to luxury print journals:

- **Playfair Display** provides the high-fashion identity. Headline scales prioritize graceful proportions, fluid italic inflections for accents, and balanced vertical rhythm. Display sizes use subtle negative tracking to preserve letterform tension.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides functional balance. Its modern geometric profile and generous apertures maintain high legibility across product specifications, ingredient breakdowns, shade names, and transactional pathways.
- **All-Caps Micro Labels (`label-caps`)** are applied to category tickers, product tags, swatch shade names, and metadata, spaced with wide letter tracking (`0.12em`) to mirror luxury packaging typography.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system implements a 12-column responsive fluid grid structured around intentional negative space:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `gutter: 1.5rem` and broad outer margin of `margin: 4rem` (or max-width container of 1440px centered). Product detail pages, lookbooks, and editorial features celebrate expansive pauses in content flow.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `gutter: 1.25rem` and `margin: 2rem`. Product grids collapse from 4 columns to 2 or 3 columns.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns with `gutter: 1rem` and `margin: 1.25rem`. Complex editorial side-by-sides stack vertically, while curated shade pickers and product carousel ribbons allow edge-to-edge horizontal panning.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile and warm, avoiding generic cool dropshadows in favor of soft ambient radiance and surface layering:

- **Surface Tiers:** Natural depth is achieved by stacking tones: Base Alabaster (`#FDFAF7`) carries containers in Warm Nude (`#F9F5F0`) and elevated overlays in pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Luminous Micro-Outlines:** Cards, floating navigation pills, and dialog panels utilize a 1px micro-border in `#EADBDB` (or `rgba(26, 20, 18, 0.06)`).
- **Ambient Blush Shadow:** Elevated interactive panels (floating cart drawer, modal popovers, dropdowns) cast an ultra-diffused, tinted shadow: `0 18px 40px -12px rgba(42, 33, 30, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(183, 122, 101, 0.04)`.
- **Glass Blurs:** Floating sticky bars and luxury navigation tiers use a frosted glass effect: `background: rgba(253, 250, 247, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces refined organic curves inspired by cosmetic compacts, polished glass flacons, and sculpted lipstick bullets:

- **Interactive Primary Elements:** Buttons, search triggers, pill badges, and seasonal tags utilize fully rounded pill silhouettes (`roundedness: 3` / 9999px border-radius) for an inviting, sensorial feel.
- **Cards & Surfaces:** Product cards, modal dialogs, and media modules adopt softened corners (`1rem` to `1.5rem`), retaining structure without clinical rigidity.
- **Color Swatches:** Circular disks (`50%` radius) encased in concentric 1px indicator rings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill silhouette, solid `#1A1412` background, `#FDFAF7` text, styled with `label-caps`. Hover states subtly shift to `#2A211E` with an internal warm aura.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Full pill silhouette with a 1px border in `#1A1412` or `#B77A65`, transparent background, and high-contrast text. On hover, fills with `#F7D6CD` at 30% opacity.
- **Editorial Text Action:** Uncontained `label-caps` typography paired with a delicate animated baseline underline that expands from the center outward.

### Cards
- **Product Display Card:** Built upon `#F9F5F0` or `#FDFAF7` with an optional 1px border in `#EADBDB`. Image ratios are strictly 3:4 portrait. Content hierarchy begins with an editorial shade/category ticker, followed by the product name in `Playfair Display`, shade count indicator, and price in `price-lg`.
- **Article / Story Card:** Large editorial canvas emphasizing curated photography, overlaid with floating pill badges and italicized Playfair Display sub-headers.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Formula Badges:** Pill-shaped, delicate padding (`0.25rem 0.75rem`), `#FDFAF7` fill, 1px border in `#EADBDB`, rendered in `label-caps` (`Clean Formula`, `Award Winner`, `Limited Edition`).
- **Filter Chips:** Pill shape with `#F9F5F0` background; active state switches to `#1A1412` fill with white text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Minimalist line or enclosed soft-pill container. Default state displays a 1px border in `#EADBDB`; focused state transitions cleanly to `#B77A65` without harsh rings. Placeholder text is rendered in `body-md` muted espresso (`rgba(26, 20, 18, 0.45)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular forms. Checked state features a solid `#1A1412` fill with an ivory checkmark or centered ivory dot.

### Specialized Beauty Components
- **Shade Finder & Swatch Matrix:** Concentric 28px circular disks displaying actual pigment hex values or textures. Selected shade is ringed with an offset 1px halo in `#B77A65`.
- **Finish & Intensity Slider:** Minimalist hairline track in `#EADBDB` with a polished rose-gold circular handle (`#B77A65`).
- **Formula Accordion:** Clean horizontal dividers with 1px `#EADBDB` rule lines, collapsible drawers for INCI ingredient disclosures, and provenance notes.
